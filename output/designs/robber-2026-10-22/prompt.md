# Räuber-Spielstein

Erstellt mit dem eingebauten Imagegen-Werkzeug. Charaktervorlage: angehängter Räuber; Perspektive, Papiermaterial und Sockel: vorhandener Ritter-Spielstein. Neutraler Spielstein ohne Spielerfarbvarianten. Transparente PNG-Datei: `public/pieces/papiercollage/robber.png`. Im Spielbrett und in der Bewegungsvorschau als neutraler Räuber eingebunden.

## Vollständiger Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept / style-transfer.
Create ONE production board-game sprite for the robber in Neuland.
Input 1 is the USER'S ROBBER CHARACTER reference: preserve its unmistakable charcoal gray hood, deep shadow completely concealing the face, layered angular dark cloak, brown leather cross-body strap with square gold buckle, brown belt with square gold buckle and small belt pouch, brown wrist guards and boots. Keep the same character identity and neutral charcoal/brown/gold palette. No added sword, shield, red garments or knight helmet.
Input 2 is the EXISTING KNIGHT GAME PIECE: use it ONLY to match camera, paper-craft construction, lighting, footprint, scale, pedestal and overall production quality, not its character or colors.
CRITICAL: transform the front-facing robber reference into the SAME STEEP OVERHEAD ORTHOGRAPHIC viewpoint as the existing knight. Camera looking downward about 65 degrees below horizontal (25 degrees away from straight vertical). Show much of the TOP of the hood, shoulders and cloak with strongly foreshortened torso and legs. The face opening remains partly visible as featureless deep shadow, but DO NOT tip the character up toward the viewer to show a frontal face. Not a front elevation or eye-level view.
Handcrafted three-dimensional layered paper miniature with torn cardstock edges, visible natural paper fibers, overlapping paper pieces, elegant dimensional folds, dark gray surfaces with enough lighter edge detail to remain readable when small. Soft light from upper left, matching the knight.
The robber stands on a tiny compact round pale stone-paper pedestal like the knight, in a calm stable pose, both hands by the sides, cloak trailing only slightly so the footprint remains compact. All parts including feet and base visible. Single object centered with a generous transparent margin, filling about 85% of canvas, approximately square composition.
Genuinely transparent background with alpha. No opaque background, no checkerboard baked into pixels, no scene, no landscape, no text, no labels, no second figure, no cast shadow outside the silhouette. This is a neutral robber piece, not a player-color variant.
