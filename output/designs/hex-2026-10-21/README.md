# Hex-Felder – drei Designbeispiele

Erstellt am 21.10.2026 mit dem integrierten image_gen-Werkzeug.

- 01-handgemalt.png: detailreiche, handgemalte Landschaften.
- 02-miniatur.png: plastische, modellierte Miniaturlandschaften.
- 03-papiercollage.png: geschichtete Papierflächen mit dezenter Textur.

Jedes Beispiel zeigt Wald, Lehm, Weide, Acker, Gebirge und Wüste. Gestaltung orientiert sich an docs/GRAFIKEN.md: Sechsecke mit Spitze oben und unten, freie Mitte für Zahlenchips, einheitliches Licht von oben links.

Die Dateien sind Designübersichten zur Stilauswahl, keine direkt einsetzbaren Einzelkacheln. Für die Umsetzung werden aus dem ausgewählten Stil sechs separate, transparente Grafiken mit exaktem Hex-Ausschnitt erstellt. Die Anwendung wurde nicht verändert.

## Vollständige Generierungs-Prompts

### 01-handgemalt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: design exploration contact sheet for hexagonal terrain tile graphics in the browser board game Neuland.
Create ONE polished landscape example image showing exactly SIX LARGE terrain hexagons, arranged in a spacious three-column by two-row grid on a warm ivory background matching #f3ecdc. This is a visual design proposal, not a screenshot or UI.
Each tile has a mathematically regular POINTY-TOP hexagonal footprint: vertex at 12 o'clock and 6 o'clock, two vertical sides, width/height ratio 0.866. Straight top-down orthographic view of the ground plane, not a perspective-foreshortened hexagon. Same size and scale for every tile, fully visible, ample spacing.
Terrain arrangement and small neat German captions underneath each tile, separate from artwork: top row "Wald", "Lehm", "Weide"; bottom row "Acker", "Gebirge", "Wüste".
Wald: deep green conifer forest with a soft grassy clearing. Lehm: russet terracotta clay hills, exposed clay strata and a few clay stones, clearly red-earth terrain. Weide: lighter fresh green pasture with three small recognizable white sheep grazing toward the edges. Acker: golden ripe wheat fields with curved crop rows and a few wheat clusters at the edges. Gebirge: cool slate-gray rocky mountains and small snow caps concentrated at the perimeter. Wüste: pale warm sand dunes with sparse stones and a small cactus near the edge.
Every tile keeps a naturally quiet unobstructed central area about 35% of its width for a number chip to be added later. This must be natural matching terrain, NOT a white hole, circle, ring, token, or UI graphic. Key details stay away from the center. Terrain fills its full hexagon to all edges. Do NOT add a rim or outline around tiles, no separate base/platform, no land sticking out beyond the hex footprint. Consistent soft illumination from upper left.
Rich readable silhouettes and distinct terrain values that remain readable at small game-board scale. The entire sheet is one cohesive style. No interface, no buildings, no roads, no dice, no number chips, no watermarks, no logos, no other text except specified header and six captions.
Style/medium: elegant hand-painted gouache board-game illustration with fine paper grain, softly layered brushwork, lush organic vegetation and charming illustrated natural details. Warm, inviting, crafted, polished art direction. Light terrain relief conveyed by painted shading only. Detailed enough to feel alive but not noisy. Natural earth colors match existing game: dark forest green, pale meadow green, terracotta, wheat gold, slate gray, sand beige. Header at top, tasteful dark-brown typography, verbatim: "01 · Handgemalt".
```
